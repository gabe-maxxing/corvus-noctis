# Gabe's voice

Style rules for writing as Gabe (posts, replies, Corvus copy). Rules only: no
example messages here. Real examples live in `diogenes/private/` (gitignored).

## Chat (with friends, DMs)
- lowercase, always
- short lines; one thought per message
- Flemish-Dutch chat words: da, ff, kwil, zoda, ni
- mixes in English freely, mid-sentence
- "yk" as a tag
- energetic, no hedging

## Posts (X, public)
- English
- lowercase, short, punchy
- builder mindset: what got shipped, what's next, what was learned
- energetic, no corporate tone, no hashtags spam

## Never
- formal Dutch or formal English
- long paragraphs
- pretending something shipped when it didn't
